About

Wattlab is an Android app that measures how fast your phone actually charges with the current charger and cable, explains why a charge is slow, ranks your charger and cable setups, and tracks battery health over time.

  • Live charging power, current, voltage and temperature
  • A 60-second speed test with results you can compare
  • Your charger and cable setups ranked side by side
  • Charge records, and battery health estimates that show how sure they are

Battery data stays on your device. Wattlab has no account and no server.

关于

Wattlab 是一款 Android 应用，用于测量手机在当前充电器和数据线下的实际充电速度，解释充电慢的原因，为你的充电器和数据线组合排名，并长期跟踪电池健康。

  • 实时充电功率、电流、电压和温度
  • 60 秒测速，结果可以相互比较
  • 充电器和数据线组合并排排名
  • 充电记录，以及标明可信程度的电池健康估算

电池数据保存在你的设备上。Wattlab 没有账号，也没有服务器。
